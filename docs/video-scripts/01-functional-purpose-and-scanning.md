# Presenter 1 — Functional: purpose and scanning

**Your job:** explain the problem, who the app helps, and one useful result.
**Your slot:** 0:04–0:44, maximum 40 seconds.

## Read aloud

> Hi, I'm [Name]. Fly Allergy Aware helps travelers with food allergies prepare
> for trips, check ingredient information, and communicate their needs. It's
> designed for people with allergies and the families helping them.
>
> Here, Alex's profile tracks a peanut allergy. I photograph an ingredient label
> and check the recognized text against the package. The app flags peanuts and
> shows the evidence behind its result. It supports reading the label; it does
> not guarantee food is safe.
>
> My contribution was [your actual contribution in 8–12 words].

## Match the screen to your words

| Approximate time within your clip | Show |

| 0–10 seconds | Your face/name, then the four app tabs |
| 10–18 seconds | Profile → Profiles → Alex Demo; show peanuts and Strict |

Record the taps separately if necessary and edit out the wait for the photo
picker. The ingredient photo must genuinely list peanuts. Do not substitute a
barcode result for the photographed-label workflow in this narration.

## Know this before recording

The profile determines what is checked. A result is evidence from the available
text and data, not permission to eat something. Describe your real contribution;
you do not need to claim you wrote the scanner to present its functionality.
